# RAG Take-Home Document Pack

This folder contains the source documents for the Lightweight RAG System assignment.

## Documents

1. `pdpc_key_concepts_extract.md`  
   General PDPA concepts, including personal data, consent, deemed consent, exceptions, access/correction, protection, retention, breach notification, and accountability.

2. `pdpc_healthcare_sector_extract.md`  
   Healthcare-specific PDPA scenarios, including medical care, referrals, family medical history, teaching use, emergencies, research, access/correction, and Do Not Call examples.

3. `pdpc_basic_anonymisation_extract.md`  
   Anonymisation and de-identification concepts, including direct identifiers, indirect identifiers, k-anonymity, re-identification risk, safeguards, and anonymisation techniques.

4. `synthetic_internal_policy_addendum.md`  
   Synthetic internal policy for this assessment. It covers patient-level data, internal analytics, small-cell suppression, external sharing, AI/ML use, RAG controls, and approval requirements.

## Source hierarchy

The PDPC extracts provide general public guidance.

The synthetic internal policy addendum represents the company’s internal policy position for this assessment.

Where the internal addendum is more specific than the general PDPC guidance, treat the internal addendum as the applicable internal rule for this assignment.
